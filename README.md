# 📚 Bookstore Inventory & Sales Analytics System (Python Project)

A complete *Bookstore Management System* built using *Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn*.  
This project follows an *Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)* structure and demonstrates concepts like data handling, visualization, and control structures.  
Perfect for college mini-projects or portfolio demonstrations.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🧾 Inventory Management
- Add new books with title, author, genre, price, and quantity  
- Update existing book stock (add or remove quantity)  
- Remove books permanently from inventory  
- Auto-detect and alert for *low stock (<20 units)*  

### 💰 Sales & Revenue Tracking
- Record sales transactions  
- Automatically deduct sold books from inventory  
- Auto-generate total revenue and sales reports  

### 📊 Data Analytics (Using Pandas & NumPy)
- Total revenue (NumPy integration)
- Average book price
- Top 5 best-selling books
- Monthly revenue & sales growth rate
- Revenue distribution by genre

### 📈 Data Visualization (Using Matplotlib & Seaborn)
- Bar chart: Total books sold by genre  
- Line chart: Monthly sales and growth trend  
- Pie chart: Revenue share by genre  
- Heatmap: Correlation between price and sales
